3 money mistakes that fail club audits
And how to avoid them before your year-end.
1 / 5
2 / 5
MISTAKE 1
Counting transfers as income
Moving LKR 10,000 from the admin account to the projects account is not new money. Logged as "income" and "expense", it inflates both totals and the auditor will ask why.
THE FIX
Record it as a move. Balances change place; income and spending stay true.
3 / 5
MISTAKE 2
Mixing club and project money
Dues pay for running the club. Fundraising pays for projects. Spend one on the other without a record and your fund statements won't add up.
THE FIX
Tag every entry to a fund. If project money pays a club bill, note that projects are owed it back.
4 / 5
MISTAKE 3
Paying from your own pocket, then forgetting
A member pays the hall from their own bank. If you deduct it from the club account straight away, your books won't match the bank statement.
THE FIX
Record it as the club owes them. The bank only goes down when you pay them back.
