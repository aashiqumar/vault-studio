# Vault social strategy

## Goal
Rotaract treasurers and presidents book a 15-minute demo. Secondary: become *the* page treasurers save for money tips.

## Audience
Rotaract club treasurers, presidents and secretaries, plus district officers, mostly aged 18–30, in Sri Lanka. Mostly on phones, in the evening. Members are not accountants.

## Content pillars and target mix
| Pillar | Target | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| tips (Dot's Tips) | 35% | Saves and shares; authority |
| product | 30% | Show the app working |
| community | 15% | Polls, Q&A, club stories, behind the scenes |
| problem | 10% | Relatable pain points; hooks |
| brand | 5% | Dot, logo, milestones |
| conversion | 5% | Demo, webinar, offers |

## Cadence
- **Feed:** 3 posts a week. Mon is a Dot's Tip or carousel, Wed a reel, Fri product or community.
- **Stories:** 4–5 days a week (polls, quizzes, "Ask Dot", behind the scenes, reposts).
- **Times (Sri Lanka):** 7:30pm on weekdays, 12:30pm as a backup, stories through the evening.
- **Grid rhythm:** alternate backgrounds; never 3 of the same in a row. Rose backgrounds are sparing (covers only).

## KPIs (review weekly in `content/performance.md`)
- **Saves + shares per post:** the main quality signal for tips.
- **Reach, profile visits, link clicks, demo requests:** the funnel.
- **Starting targets for the end of launch week 4:** 300 followers, 40 link clicks a week, 10 demo requests. Re-set after week 2.

## Paid (suggested starting point)
- **Phase 1:** boost the best reel at about LKR 1,500 a day for 7 days, targeting Sri Lanka, ages 18–34, interests volunteering, university clubs, leadership.
- **Retargeting:** about LKR 1,000 a day for 10 days on the carousel and demo post, to reel viewers and profile visitors.
- **Kill rule:** if cost per demo request looks bad by day 4, move the budget to the post with the most saves.

## Seasonal hooks (Sri Lanka and Rotaract year)
- **July:** new boards and treasurer handover.
- **Aug–Sep:** planning and budgets.
- **Oct–Nov:** project season.
- **Dec:** holiday projects and year-end for calendar-year clubs.
- **Jan–Feb:** mid-year review.
- **Mar:** Rotaract week (no RI marks).
- **Apr:** Avurudu.
- **May:** Vesak.
- **June:** audits and the year-end rush (peak season for tips and demos).
